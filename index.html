What is the 21-Day Transformation Programme?

A guided challenge focused on fitness, nutrition and daily habit building, supported through WhatsApp.

Do I need to be fit before joining?

No. The programme is designed around practical, scalable habits. Exact fitness guidance should be based on the coach's approved programme.

How does WhatsApp work?

After registration, you'll continue to WhatsApp where the onboarding and daily challenge flow can be automated through the client's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Can I join another challenge later?

Yes. The platform is designed so the coach can create different 7, 12, 14 or 21-day programmes.
